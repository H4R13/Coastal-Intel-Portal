Formula and assumptions

Index(year) = 100 × (1 + annualGrowth / 100)^(year − 2020)
Increase (%) = (Index(year) / 100 − 1) × 100

2020 is a normalized baseline, not an observed measurement. All three growth rates and the category thresholds are demonstration assumptions, not official limits. Constant compound growth does not account for cleanup, storms, river discharge, transport, seasonality or policy changes. The graphic is an artistic schematic: particle count, spacing, speed, settlements and habitat symbols are not physical measurements or real mapped locations. This component does not simulate geographic movement or estimate biomagnification. The impact cards show general potential pathways, not confirmed Indus Delta damages, health outcomes or affected-person counts.

To obtain a data-based forecast, replace these assumptions with consistent Indus Delta measurements and a calibrated model. Total debris growth alone cannot establish ecological or public-health risk.
